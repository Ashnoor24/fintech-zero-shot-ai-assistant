# Task 3: Evidence-Based Summarization — Prompts

## 1. Initial Prompt
Summarise the following passage in 3 bullet points and list what cannot be concluded:

"PaySetu, a fictional payments start-up, processed 4.2 lakh transactions in September and 5.1 lakh in October. Average transaction value fell from ₹640 to ₹590. Server downtime increased in October. No data is available on revenue or costs."

## 2. Revised Prompt (Strict Grounding)
Act as a financial operations analyst reviewing a short internal report.

Read the passage below and produce two sections:
1. **Factual Summary:** Exactly 3 bullet points stating only facts directly present in the text.
2. **What Cannot Be Concluded:** A bulleted list of business metrics, causes, or outcomes that cannot be inferred from the passage.

Strict Rules:
- Use only the exact numbers and facts given in the passage.
- Do not speculate on causes (for example, do not link server downtime to transaction value).
- Do not make claims about revenue, profit, margins, or customer satisfaction.

Passage:
"PaySetu, a fictional payments start-up, processed 4.2 lakh transactions in September and 5.1 lakh in October. Average transaction value fell from ₹640 to ₹590. Server downtime increased in October. No data is available on revenue or costs."
